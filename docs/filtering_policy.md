# Filtering Policy

## Scope

This document is the project-level filtering policy for future waveform parsing and signal
processing in `nhtsa_metadata_side_loadcell`.

Current analysis-ready files are metadata and channel-inventory surfaces only. They do not parse
waveforms and do not apply filters yet.

## Default Filters

| Physical quantity | Default filter |
|---|---|
| side pole load-cell individual force | CFC 60 |
| side pole load-cell total force | CFC 60 |
| VEHICLE CG acceleration | CFC 60 |
| velocity/displacement integration acceleration | CFC 180 |
| velocity/displacement result | CFC 180 |
| raw waveform | Preserve unfiltered |

## Required Data Policy

- Raw waveform must be preserved unfiltered.
- Filtered force, acceleration, velocity, and displacement are derived signals.
- Derived signals must preserve lineage back to `test_no + curve_no`.
- Timebase reconstruction must use channel metadata such as `NFP`, `NLP`, and `DELT` when waveform data is parsed.
- Filtering must not overwrite raw source waveform files or raw parsed arrays.
